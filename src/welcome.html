FFillsmith

Installed. Nothing runs until you ask it to.

Fillsmith fills the form on the page you are looking at with data that belongs to one invented person, reads every control back, and says what it did.

  1. Open a page with a formAny page: a staging admin, a sign-up form, a checkout in test mode.
  2. Press Alt+Shift+F, or the Fillsmith icon, then Fill this page Pin the icon in the toolbar puzzle menu if you want it in reach. A card in the corner of the page says how many fields were filled and what was left.
  3. Press Alt+Shift+R for a different person, Alt+Shift+C to clear Right-click a field for Fill just this field, or press Alt+Shift+D with the cursor in it. Settings has the data language and, when a bug needs the same data twice, a way to repeat a fill.

The on-device model is optional: without it the rules fill every field they recognise. If Chrome offers it, the popup shows a download button — one time, about two gigabytes, on your machine.
